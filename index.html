Ask your question ↗
Are you a distributor or an investment adviser?

We are a distributor. AGM Wealth is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-124172) and an APMI-registered PMS Distributor (APRN 00099), and we also distribute AIF and SIF products and insurance. We are not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser, so we do not give personalised investment advice. We explain products, costs and risks so that you can decide.

How does AGM Wealth earn?

We earn commission from the product providers, such as fund houses, PMS and AIF managers, insurers and issuers. Mutual fund investments through us are in regular plans, which include a distributor commission within the scheme’s expenses. Details are on our Commission Disclosures page.

Can I begin the onboarding online?

Yes. Download the AGM Wealth app to start registration and KYC (PAN and Aadhaar are needed). If you prefer help, message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you through it.

Can you help if I already have investments?

Yes. Share your Consolidated Account Statement and we will go through what you hold: overlap, concentration, costs and liquidity. It is a conversation about your holdings, not a recommendation to switch. You can start with the Portfolio Analyzer.

Do you work with investors outside Jaipur?

Yes. Our clients are across India and abroad, onboarded digitally. NRI investors, including those in the US and Canada, can invest with select fund houses that accept them, subject to FATCA compliance and each fund house’s own conditions.

Frequently Asked

A few common questions

What's the difference between a distributor and an investment adviser?

A distributor (what AGM Wealth is) helps you access and execute mutual fund, PMS, AIF, and other regulated products, earning commission disclosed transparently. A SEBI-registered Investment Adviser charges a fee for personalised advice and cannot earn product commission. We're upfront about being a distributor, not an adviser.

Is there a minimum amount to start a SIP?

Most mutual fund SIPs can start from as low as ₹500/month, though this varies by scheme. PMS, AIF, and SIF carry much higher minimums set by SEBI regulation - we can walk you through which applies where.

How do you get paid?

We earn trail commission from AMCs on Regular Plan investments - fully disclosed on our Commission Disclosures page. We don't charge you a separate fee on top, and we don't earn anything on Direct Plans.

Can I switch from Direct Plans to investing through you, or vice versa?

Yes, both directions are possible, though switching between Direct and Regular Plans may involve tax implications (treated as a redemption and fresh purchase) and possibly exit loads. We're happy to walk through the specifics for your situation.

Do you provide services outside Jaipur?

Yes - while our office and in-person meetings are based in Jaipur, we service clients across India digitally through our app and video calls.
