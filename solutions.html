What We Distribute

Investment solutions, matched to your goals

AGM Wealth is a distributor, not an adviser - we help you access and execute the following SEBI-regulated products. Suitability depends on your goals, risk tolerance, and (for PMS/AIF) minimum investment thresholds.

MF

Mutual Funds & SIPs

AMFI Registered MFD · ARN 124172

Mutual funds pool money from many investors into a diversified, professionally managed portfolio - reducing single-stock risk and putting decisions in the hands of qualified fund managers.

Who May Consider It

Most investors - from first-time SIP starters to those building a diversified core portfolio across equity, debt, and hybrid categories.

What to Evaluate

Fund category, expense ratio, exit load, fund manager track record, and how the scheme fits your goal and time horizon.

PMS

Portfolio Management Services

APMI Registered PMS Distributor · APRN 00099

A portfolio managed by a SEBI-registered portfolio manager according to a defined strategy, while the underlying securities are generally held in the investor's own name.

Who May Consider It

Eligible investors seeking a focused, professionally managed portfolio and comfortable with market volatility and strategy-specific risk.

What to Evaluate

Investment approach, concentration, drawdowns, fees, liquidity, taxation, reporting and the manager's track record across market cycles.

AIF

Alternative Investment Funds

AIF Distributor · AIF 124172

A privately pooled investment vehicle managed under a stated mandate, spanning Category I, II and III strategies across private markets, credit, real assets and market-linked approaches.

Who May Consider It

Eligible, informed investors seeking exposure beyond conventional mutual funds and able to accept complexity, longer horizons and limited liquidity.

What to Evaluate

Fund category, strategy, manager experience, lock-in and exit terms, valuation, fees, leverage, concentration and taxation.

SIF

Specialised Investment Funds

AMFI Registered SIF Distributor · SIFD 124172

A specialised mutual-fund framework permitting differentiated strategies with greater flexibility than conventional mutual funds, within its regulatory framework.

Who May Consider It

Informed investors who understand the strategy, have appropriate risk capacity and can remain invested for the recommended horizon.

What to Evaluate

Strategy and asset mix, risk band, use of derivatives, liquidity, benchmark, costs, exit load and the Scheme Information Document.

BND

Bonds & Debentures

Fixed Income Distribution

Lend money to a government body or company in exchange for periodic interest and repayment of principal at maturity - a lower-risk complement to equity holdings.

Who May Consider It

Investors prioritising capital stability, predictable income, and portfolio diversification against equity market swings.

What to Evaluate

Issuer creditworthiness, credit rating, tenure, interest rate risk, and liquidity before investing.

SN

Structured Notes

Market-Linked Debentures

Hybrid instruments linking returns to an underlying asset (an equity index, stock basket, or commodity) - issued by financial institutions with a defined payoff structure and tenure.

Who May Consider It

Investors comfortable with product complexity, seeking customised risk-return profiles not available through standard mutual funds or bonds.

What to Evaluate

Payoff structure, underlying asset, issuer credit risk, capital protection (if any), liquidity before maturity, and tax treatment.

LIFE

Life Insurance

ICICI Prudential Life Insurance · Agency ICI01334492

Financial protection for your family in the event of your passing - term plans, endowment, and ULIPs depending on your goals and stage of life.

Who May Consider It

Every earning member with financial dependents, particularly those with loans or young children who'd be affected by a loss of income.

What to Evaluate

Sum assured adequacy, claim settlement track record, premium payment term, and whether a pure term plan meets your protection need more efficiently than an investment-linked policy.

HLTH

Health Insurance

Care Health Insurance Limited · Agency RLH20076171

Coverage for hospitalisation, surgeries, and medical expenses - a foundational protection layer independent of your employer's group cover.

Who May Consider It

Individuals and families without adequate employer health cover, or wanting cover that continues after a job change.

What to Evaluate

Room rent limits, sub-limits on specific treatments, waiting periods for pre-existing conditions, and network hospital coverage.

GEN

General Insurance

Tata AIG General Insurance Co. Ltd · Agency AIG2298010000

Motor, home, and travel cover - protecting the assets and trips that sit outside your investment and life-protection planning.

Who May Consider It

Vehicle owners, homeowners, and frequent travellers wanting protection against accidents, damage, theft, or trip disruptions.

What to Evaluate

Coverage exclusions, deductibles, add-on riders (e.g. zero-depreciation for motor), and claim process convenience.
