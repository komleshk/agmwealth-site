Step 2: Escalate to AMFI

If your grievance is not resolved to your satisfaction, you can escalate it to AMFI's Standing Committee on Certified Distributors:

  • AMFI website: www.amfiindia.com
  • Reference our ARN (124172) when filing your complaint

Step 3: Escalate to SEBI SCORES

For unresolved grievances related to securities market intermediaries, you may file a complaint with SEBI's SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System) platform:

  • SCORES portal: scores.sebi.gov.in
  • SCORES enables direct online filing and tracking of your complaint with SEBI

Step 4: ODR Platform (Online Dispute Resolution)

SEBI has also enabled a common Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal for securities market disputes, offering conciliation and arbitration as an alternative to traditional grievance redressal.

  • ODR portal: smartodr.in
We encourage you to reach out to us directly first (Step 1) - most concerns can be resolved quickly through a direct conversation.
