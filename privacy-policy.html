Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026 · Applies to agmwealth.com and the AGM Wealth mobile application (Android & iOS)

This Privacy Policy sets out how AGM Wealth ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information shared through our website and mobile application. AGM Wealth is committed to protecting your privacy. Where we ask you to provide information by which you can be identified, that information will only be used in accordance with this policy.

Our relationship with you is built on trust. In the course of providing services, we may become privy to personal information of a confidential nature. We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of this information and its transmission over the internet, though we cannot be liable for disclosures required by law, regulation, or judicial/regulatory order.

Information We Collect

Name and Contact Details

We collect personal information directly from you - such as your name, email address, phone number, PAN, and other KYC identifiers - when you register an account, complete KYC, or use application features. This is used to provide access to app functionality, personalize your experience, and communicate about your account or transactions.

Camera, Photo Gallery & Document Data

With your permission, the app may access your device's camera or photo gallery to enable document capture and upload for Video KYC and identity verification. Images you capture or upload are used specifically for document verification and are not shared with third parties except where required by law or necessary to complete a service you've requested.

Location Data

We access location data solely to help verify your identity during KYC and to grant access to location-dependent application features.

Financial & Transaction Data

Investment holdings, transaction history, and portfolio data are collected and processed to provide portfolio tracking, reporting, and transaction execution services.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to the application's features and functionality
  • To process KYC, SIP registration, purchase, and redemption requests
  • To personalize your portfolio dashboard and reports
  • To communicate with you about your account, transactions, or service updates
  • To meet regulatory, legal, and audit obligations

Data Sharing

Your data is not shared with third parties except: (a) with Asset Management Companies (AMCs), Registrars and Transfer Agents (RTAs such as CAMS/KFintech), and other service providers strictly to execute your transactions; (b) with regulators, government, or judicial bodies where legally required; or (c) where you have explicitly requested a transaction that necessitates disclosure.

Your Rights

You can review, update, or correct your personal information at any time through the app's account settings, or by contacting us directly. You may request deletion of your account or specific personal information, subject to regulatory record-keeping requirements applicable to financial intermediaries (SEBI/AMFI mandate minimum retention periods for KYC and transaction records that we must comply with even after an account is closed).

Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communications are encrypted using 256-bit encryption. Passwords are one-way encrypted before storage. Data is hosted with reputable hosting providers and backed up continuously to ensure service continuity.

Third-Party Links

Our website and app may contain links to third-party sites. We do not control these sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults capable of entering into a binding investment relationship under Indian law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you believe any information we hold about you is incorrect or incomplete, or if you have questions about this policy, please email us at agmwealth1@gmail.com or call +91 88750 50447. We will promptly correct any information found to be inaccurate.
