Founder's Journey

Kamlesh Mandowara

Founder, AGM Wealth · Mutual Fund, SIF, PMS & AIF Distributor

Kamlesh brings 25+ years of total professional experience, including 19+ years in banking and financial services. Prior to entering financial services, he spent six years in the corporate pharmaceutical sector (May 2001 – August 2007), developing disciplined operational and client management practices.

In September 2007, he transitioned to private banking as a Preferred Banking Relationship Manager. In September 2010, he joined ICICI Bank's wealth management division as an Investment Specialist, advising affluent families on portfolio structuring, mutual funds, and fixed-income assets.

He later led banking branches as Branch Manager at ICICI Bank (April 2014 – January 2016) and Kotak Mahindra Bank (January 2016 – April 2017). Drawing on this extensive institutional background, he founded AGM Wealth in April 2017 to provide families with transparent, disciplined distribution and personal long-term partnership.
