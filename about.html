Meet the Founder

Kamlesh Mandowara

Founder, AGM Wealth · Mutual Fund, SIF, PMS & AIF Distributor

Kamlesh began his banking career in September 2007 as a Preferred Banking Relationship Manager. In September 2010, he moved to ICICI Bank's wealth-management division as an Investment Specialist, working closely with clients on their investment needs before taking on branch leadership responsibilities.

He served as a Branch Manager at ICICI Bank from April 2014 to January 2016 and at Kotak Mahindra Bank from January 2016 to April 2017. Drawing on a decade of banking and wealth-management experience, he founded AGM Wealth in April 2017 to help families make financial decisions with greater clarity, discipline and personal attention.
