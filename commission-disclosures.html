Commission Disclosures

Disclosure under SEBI Circular SEBI/IMD/CIR No.4/168230/09 · Last updated: 21 July 2026

As an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-124172), AGM Wealth earns trail commission from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) on the Regular Plan investments we facilitate. We do not charge investors a separate advisory or distribution fee on top of this - our compensation comes entirely from the AMCs, as disclosed below.

We deal exclusively in Regular Plans. Direct Plans, which carry a lower expense ratio because they exclude distributor commission, are available to any investor directly through the respective AMC or via platforms such as MF Utility or individual AMC websites. We do not earn any commission on Direct Plan investments.
