A Mutual Fund Distributor in Jaipur, Registered Since Day One

AGM Wealth is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-124172) in Jaipur, helping investors build disciplined, goal-based portfolios through SIPs and lumpsum investments.

What a Mutual Fund Distributor Does

A mutual fund pools money from many investors into a diversified, professionally managed portfolio of stocks, bonds, or a mix of both - run by a qualified fund manager, not you individually picking stocks. As your distributor, our role is to help you access, compare, and execute the right schemes for your goals, complete KYC, and stay invested through the full cycle - not to manage the fund itself, which the AMC's fund manager does.

SIP vs. Lumpsum in Jaipur's Investing Culture

Jaipur's business community has historically favoured tangible assets - gold, property, fixed deposits - over market-linked instruments. A monthly SIP fits that same disciplined, patient instinct extremely well: a fixed amount, invested on a fixed date, regardless of market mood, building a habit rather than requiring you to time the market. For a lump sum already in hand - from a sale, bonus, or inheritance - a considered lumpsum or staggered deployment may suit better. We'll walk through which fits your specific situation, using our SIP and Lumpsum calculators to make the numbers concrete rather than abstract.

Who Mutual Funds Suit

Most investors - from a first SIP starting at ₹500/month to a family building a diversified core portfolio across equity, debt, and hybrid categories over a multi-year horizon.

What to Evaluate Before Choosing

Fund category and mandate, expense ratio, exit load, the fund manager's track record across market cycles, and how the specific scheme actually fits your goal and time horizon - not just recent returns.

Regular Plans, and Why We Disclose What We Earn

We distribute Regular Plans and earn trail commission from AMCs - disclosed in full on our Commission Disclosures page, by scheme category. Direct Plans, which carry a lower expense ratio because they exclude distributor commission, are available to any investor directly through the AMC; we don't earn anything on those. We think that distinction is worth knowing before you invest, not after.
