  • Distribution registration by AGM Wealth (ARN-124172) is not an assurance of returns and does not constitute a recommendation of suitability for any particular investor.

Product-Specific Risk Notes

Mutual Funds

Subject to market risk. Equity-oriented schemes carry higher volatility than debt-oriented schemes. Please evaluate exit loads, total expense ratio (TER), and other costs before investing.

Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

PMS involves concentrated portfolios and manager discretion, which can result in higher volatility than diversified mutual funds. Minimum investment thresholds and lock-in/exit terms apply as per the manager's disclosure document.

Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)

AIFs may carry higher risk, illiquidity, and lock-in periods depending on category and strategy. Returns are not guaranteed and can vary significantly.

Bonds & Debentures

Subject to credit risk (issuer default), interest rate risk, and liquidity risk. Lower-rated instruments carry higher risk of default.

Insurance

Insurance products are subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and waiting periods specified in the respective policy document. Please read the policy wording carefully before purchase.

Please consider your specific investment requirements, and consult a qualified professional where appropriate, before making any investment decision.
